## Make your video

1. Pick one well-lit photo of yourself or another adult who has agreed. Keep the face, hair and upper body visible. Avoid group photos, sunglasses and blur. Use JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or HEIF up to 20 MB.
2. Open the [generator](https://bulin47ai.space/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=guide&utm_campaign=english_tutorial_20261021#studio), select **Choose my photo** and check the local preview. Choosing a file does not start a charge or a video generation.
3. Review one video or a pack, and confirm permission and the terms. Each video is a 15-second, 720p vertical MP4 with the original sound.
4. Select **Continue with Google** before purchase. No new password is needed. Return to the original tab, check the photo and selected pack, then continue to Stripe and review the amount before paying.
5. Keep the private order link or open **My orders** with the same Google account. Generation can take several minutes. Check an existing order before making another purchase.
6. Download the finished MP4 and save a local copy within its original 30-day retention period. For packs, use **View my pack** to make the remaining videos later, one photo at a time.
